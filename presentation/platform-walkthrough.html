The worker fills a 14-day grid. That's it.

Name, position, NIS, ID, wage, COLA, allowance and bank details are pre-filled and read-only — pulled from the verified registry. The worker only touches attendance.

Top of the ladder · not a penny more or less

The recheck is a verification, not a re-entry.

By the time a sheet reaches Accounts, the numbers are already computed. The clerk's job is to confirm, not recalculate — comparing the derived figures against rates, days and bank details, then authorising or rejecting with a reason.

  • ✓Deterministic totals — same inputs always yield the same cent, so a verify is a glance, not a spreadsheet.
  • ↺Backpay is exact too — a retroactive rate change re-runs (new − old) × days per affected fortnight.
  • ⤺Reject drops it one rung with a mandatory note; the originator is notified to correct & resubmit.
Tamper-evident audit

Every change is chained.

Each mutation is logged with a full before/after diff and a SHA-256 hash of the entry before it. Alter any past record and every later hash breaks — provable in review or in court.

Where flow stalls · click a card for the fix

A pipeline is only as fast as its slowest desk.

The system flags any sheet stalled at one stage beyond a threshold (default 48h) and surfaces it on the Director's Bottlenecks tab with the responsible role attached.

Designed-in safeguards

The flow is built to keep moving.

Director's live pipeline

A horizontal count of sheets at every stage — throughput and pile-ups visible at a glance, with drill-down by corporation, stage, date or worker.

Automatic bottleneck detection

The 48-hour (configurable) threshold turns "somewhere on someone's desk" into a named list of who owns what, right now.

Batch approval

Coordinators clear a whole queue in one action instead of card-by-card, collapsing the most common backlog.

Roster defaults & caps

Mon–Fri pre-marked present and a 10-day cap mean data-entry is subtraction, not entry — and no one is paid beyond the maximum.

Escalation-ready: the same stalled-item signal that feeds the Director can drive reminders and reassignment as the programme scales.
